Hands-on Computer Networks

Read the concepts, observe packets and logs, and test your explanations. Use the materials on your own or in a study group.

This open learning project began in the fourth network study group of Aolda, the cloud operations club at Ajou University. Anyone can use it.

HOW TO STUDY

Predict, test,
then explain

  1. Read with a question. Choose one phenomenon and identify the concepts you need to explain it.
  2. Keep your reasoning. Record questions, confusing distinctions and the conditions behind your answer in your existing notes.
  3. Run the experiment. Predict the result, run the command, observe it, change one condition and restore the baseline.
  4. Compare evidence. Explain what the output supports and what remains unknown. In a group, compare your reasoning and where you got stuck.
